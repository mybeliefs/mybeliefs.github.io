# Jude

- https://www.esv.org/jude/

## Overview

> **Instructs** believers to beware of corrupt teachers

- `1` These men follow their own evil desires, keep yourselves in the love of God

#### Background:

- Brother of James; Jesus had 4 brothers (James, Jude, Joseph, Simon)
- Neither James nor Jude ever describes themselves as a brother to Jesus
- Written mid-60 AD
- Uses OT and other Jewish literature to demonstrate how rebellious people/leaders receive divine judgment

## Chapters (1)
https://www.bibleref.com/Jude/1/Jude-chapter-1.html

### Ch 1

> These men follow their own evil desires, keep yourselves in the love of God

- people have crept in ... who pervert the grace of our God into sensuality `4`
- people saved out of the land of Egypt were destroyed who did not believe `5`
- Sodom and Gomorrah indulged in sexual immorality, unnatural desire, and the punishment of external file `7`
- These men follow their own evil desires; boast about themselves `16`
- In the last time, there will be scoffers, following their own ungodly passions `18`
- keep yourselves in the love of God, waiting for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ that leads to eternal life `21`
- Have mercy on those who doubt `22`
- to him who is able to keep you (us) from stumbling `24`

## Observations

- Doxology 24-25:
  > *Now to him who is able to keep you from stumbling and to present you blameless before the presence of his glory with great joy, to the only God, our Savior, through Jesus Christ our Lord, be glory, majesty, dominion, and authority, before all time and now and forever. Amen.*
- Corrupt teachers will come (we should expect it)
- People are judged for their sins: Sodom and Gomorrah
- Our belief is reflected our morals

### Difficulties

- References to [apocryphal](https://www.gotquestions.org/apocrypha-deuterocanonical.html) writings
- https://www.gotquestions.org/Michael-Satan-Moses.html
- Notes from Bible Project - Why reference books outside of the Bible?
  > "It’s important to remember that the Jewish culture of this time was immersed in religious texts. Jesus, his family, and all of the early Jewish Christians grew up reading the Hebrew Bible along with many other books based on and inspired by the Scriptures. This is no different than reading popular religious books by authors in our own day. While they’re not a part of the Bible, many other texts offer powerful messages to God’s people."

### Bible Project

https://bibleproject.com/guides/book-of-jude/

**Background**
- One of Jesus' four brothers; none of them followed Jesus until his resurrection
- Made up of Messianic Jews
- Assumes a deep understanding of the Old Testament
- Warns against corrupt teachers

**1:1-4: Contend for the True Faith**
- Corrupt teachers have got into the church.
- Not the teaching per se, but their immoral lifestyle

**1:5-19: Beware of False Teachers**
- Uses Old Testament examples of divine judgment
  - The Israelites rebelled against God in the wilderness; they never went to the promised land.
  - Angels rebel against God and have sex with women. Referring to the book of Enoch
  - In Sodom and Gomorrah, violent men try to have sex with angels.
  - Cain murdered his brother and then built a city full of violence.
  - Balaam lured Israel into idolatry and sexual misbehavior.
  - Lorah and Levite rebelled against Moses.
- Peter, John, and Paul all predicted corrupt teachers

**1:20-25: How to Contend for the Faith**
- The Church builds itself up through prayer, love of God, and obedience.
- Stay alert for the  return of Jesus.


### Bible Ref

- Brother of James, half-brother of Jesus
- Due to numerous Old Testament references written to Jewish Christians or well-informed Gentile believers.
- Date between 67 and 80
- Shares a common theme with 2 Peter
- Emphasis is on the dangers of false teachings.
