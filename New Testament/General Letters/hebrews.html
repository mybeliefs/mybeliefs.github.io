Hebrews

  • https://www.esv.org/hebrews/

Overview​

Instructs believers on how Jesus replaces the old convenent and how we should live

  • 1 The Son is God
  • 2 Warning to Pay Attention, Faithful High Priest
  • 3 Jesus greater than Moses, Do not harden your heart
  • 4 God’s rest, God’s word is living and active, Jesus is our High Priest
  • 5 Jesus is our High Priest
  • 6 Move beyond the basics, Continue helping other believers
  • 7 Perfect Priest
  • 8 New covenant
  • 9 Christ replaces the Tabernacle
  • 10Christ’s Sacrifice Once for All
  • 11 Can’t please God without Faith
  • 12 Remove sin, Be holy, Acceptable Worship
  • 13 Show love to others, Avoid strange teachings, Share with others

Background:​

  • Author unknown - maybe Barnabas, Apollos, or Paul
  • The author was a Jewish Christian, well versed in the OT (Greek translation)
  • Written before 70 AD - no mention of the destruction of the temple
  • The audience was facing persecution
  • Addressing Jewish converts being tempted to Judaize the gospel

Chapters (13)​

Ch 1​

https://www.bibleref.com/Hebrews/1/Hebrews-chapter-1.html

The Son is God

  • Summary:
    • God revealed Himself through prophets in the past. But now He reveals Himself through His Son, Jesus
    • Jesus is both the Son of God and God the Son
    • Angels are told to worship Him; only God can be worshipped
    • The Trinity
  • Long ago, our fathers spoke to us using the prophets (OT) 1
  • But now revelation through his Son, through whom he created the world 2
  • The Son 3:
    • the radiance of God’s glory
    • exact representation of his being (mirrors God)
    • sustains all things by his powerful word
    • provided purification for sins
    • sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high
  • Superior to Angels 4-13:
    • God’s angels worship the Son (only God can be worshipped) 6
    • “Your throne, O God, will last forever”. The Son is God (Trinity) 8
    • loved righteousness and hated wickedness 9
    • Created the heavens and earth 10
    • Years will never end 12
    • God said "Sit at my right hand ..." 13

https://www.bibleref.com/Hebrews/2/Hebrews-chapter-2.html

Warning to Pay Attention, Faithful High Priest

    • Suffered death for everyone
    • Has defeated the Devil

    • God also bore witness (of the message) by signs and wonders and miracles 4

  • ... because of the suffering of death, so that by the grace of God he might taste death for everyone 9
  • ... that through death he might destroy the one who has the power of death ... the devil 14

https://www.bibleref.com/Hebrews/3/Hebrews-chapter-3.html

Jesus is greater than Moses, Do not harden your heart

  • Summary:

    • Moses was a part of the House of God, but Jesus is the builder of the House
    • Moses was a servant, Jesus is the Son
    • Because of Israel’s sin, they were rejected
    • Hard hearts and unbelief always prevent God’s blessings in our lives
  • Consider Jesus, our apostle and high priest 1
  • Jesus is worthy of more glory than Moses 3
  • Do not harden your heart (the early generation did as they wandered in the desert) 8
  • Take care, don't have a sinful and unbelieving heart, leading you to fall away from the living God 12
  • Encourage one another; not being hardened by sin’s deceitfulness 13
  • They were told they (desert group) would not enter his rest 18
    • 19 Because of their unbelief 19

    • Nothing is hidden, everything is laid bare before his eyes; we must give an account 13

Ch 5​

https://www.bibleref.com/Hebrews/5/Hebrews-chapter-5.html

Jesus is our High Priest

  • Summary:
    • A priest had to be called by God.
    • Christ learned obedience by experience, suffering on the cross.
    • Was always obedient
    • The spiritual immaturity of some of the Hebrew Christians was a concern.
  • High Priest represents us 1-6
    • Priest selected to represent us before God, offering sacrifices for sin 1
    • Has his own weaknesses 2
    • Offers a sacrifice for his own sins, also 3
    • Must be called by God 4
    • Christ was appointed to be the high priest 5
  • Jesus is our high priest 7-10
    • Jesus, while on earth, offered prayers and petitions 7
    • Although he was a son, he learned obedience from what he suffered 8
    • Being made perfect, He became the source of eternal salvation for all who obey him 9
    • Christ became our high priest after the order of Melchizedek (mel.KIZ.uh.dek) 10
  • By this time, you ought to be teachers; you need someone to teach you the basic principles 12

Ch 6​

https://www.bibleref.com/Hebrews/6/Hebrews-chapter-6.html

Move beyond the basics, Continue helping other believers

  • Summary:
    • Urged to progress in their faith, beyond the basics
    • Urges to walk with God day by day
  • To move beyond basic beliefs (repentance, faith, etc.) ... to grow in maturity 1-3
  • When people experience God and then turn their backs on Him, they can’t start over. They are re-crucifying the Son of God 4-6
  • God will not forget the work we do ... love and help shown to other believers. Continue doing this. 10
  • Jesus has become the high priest forever 20

Ch 7​

https://www.bibleref.com/Hebrews/7/Hebrews-chapter-7.html

Perfect Priest

  • Summary:
    • Melchizedek is a type of Christ. Had no father or mother.
    • Remains a priest continually.
    • Abraham gave him tithes before the priesthood was instituted.
    • The Levitical system could not produce a perfect priest.
    • Jesus is the perfect priest.
    • He always intercedes for us.
    • Unlike other priests, Jesus has no sins and offers himself as a sacrifice, spotless.
  • Melchizedek 1-3
    • priest of God 1
    • king of peace 2
    • Was without father or mother, ..., resembling the Son of God. 3
  • Descendants of Levi, who were priests, had a commandment to take tithes from the people 5
  • Perfect priest 11-27
    • The Levitical system (priesthood) could not produce a perfect priest. 11
    • Because Jesus lives forever, he has a permanent priesthood 24
    • Able to save because he always lives to intercede 25
    • Unlike other priests, he does not need to offer sacrifices daily. He sacrificed once for all when he offered himself 27

Ch 8​

https://www.bibleref.com/Hebrews/8/Hebrews-chapter-8.html

New covenant

  • Summary:
    • The sanctuary is not man-made.
    • Jesus is a mediator of a better covenant.
    • The old ways (gifts and sacrifices) were only a shadow of things to come.
    • He promised Himself to bear our sins and to be our High Priest.
    • The second covenant, God deals with the hearts of people.
  • We have a high priest in heaven 1
    • a minister in holy places, the true tent that the Lord set up 2
  • The priests served as a shadow of things to come (a copy) 5
  • Found fault with the first covenant 7
  • Second covenant 8-13 - Quoting Jeremiah 31:31-34
    • The new covenant (different from the one Sinaitic covenant) 8
    • put laws in their minds and hearts 10
    • no longer required to teach others “Know the Lord”; they will all know him 11
    • remember their sins no more 12
  • By calling it “new”, the 1st one is now obsolete 13

Ch 9​

https://www.bibleref.com/Hebrews/9/Hebrews-chapter-9.html

Christ replaces the Tabernacle

  • Summary:
    • The first covenant setting containing furniture designated by God.
    • The service was conducted by a high priest.
    • Christ is the new perfect high priest - without blemish.
    • The 2nd covenant, Jesus is our Mediator, bringing eternal life
    • The old covenant was a picture of the coming new covenant.
    • The final act is eternal life.
  • The 1st covenant 1-10
    • The first convenant had regulations for worship and also an earthly sanctuary. 1
    • The first room, called the Holy Place 2
    • Another room, called the Most Holy Place, contained the ark of the covenant 3
    • Priests entered regularly into the outer room to carry on their ministry 6
    • Only the high priest entered the “inner room” once a year with blood for the sin offering 7
    • Basically a temporary arrangement; only a matter of food and drink and various ceremonial washings 10
  • Christ appears 11-28
    • Christ appeared as the high priest 11
    • Christ entered the Most Holy Place once and for all by his own blood 12
    • Offered himself unblemished to God 14
    • Without the shedding of blood, there is no forgiveness 22
    • Christ enters heaven and appears before God on our behalf 24
    • Christ was sacrificed once to take away the sins of many people. Will appear a second time to bring salvation to those who are waiting for him 28

    • There is no longer any offering for sin. Either saved through Christ or not.
    • We can come to God with boldness because we have been cleansed.
  • The law was a shadow of the good things that are coming 1

  • Was not pleased with the sacrifices (God) 8
  • A priest offers the same sacrifices, which can never take away sins. 11
  • But Christ has offered for all time one sacrifice for sins. 12
  • The covenant will be made with them. Put the laws in their hearts 16
  • Sins and lawless acts will be remembered no more 17
  • Sacrifice for sin is no longer necessary 18
  • So now we can say with confidence to enter the Most Holy Place by Christ’s blood.19
  • So now let us draw near to God with a sincere heart (Christ did all of the work, clearing the way for us) 22
  • Let us spur one another toward love and good deeds24
  • Meet with each other; encourage one another 25
  • If we continue to sin, despite this knowledge, we are left to face judgment on our own. (This is not good, we are disrespecting what was done for us; we will be held accountable) 26
  • Need to persevere, will receive what he has promised 36

Observations​

  • Christ is God 1:3-4.
  • Jesus is the new revelation of God versus the OT Prophets ... the new covenant. He is the Son of God. Created all things and made us right by being the perfect one-time only sacrifice on the cross.
  • Is our new high priest ... versus the old system ... that understands our weakness. He also became a man. We now always have access to our priest, Jesus.
  • Our hearts are hardened by sin’s deceitfulness. Just like the Jews in the desert who did not enter God’s rest. Hard hearts and unbelief often prevent God’s gracious blessings from entering our lives.
  • Without Faith you can not please God ... trusting in what we do not see. We must believe that He exists.

    “Lord help my faith. Help me see you more clearly. Allow me to believe so I can enter your rest”.

  • Blesses those who earnestly seek Him
  • Faith is being sure of what we hope for and certain of what we do not see 11:1
  • We are to fear God in a reverent way. We are to seek holiness. We need to seek God. It is easy to use God for our own agenda. No, we need to seek Him, love others, don’t love money, etc., ... pursue holiness.

    "Fear of God is good for us!"

  • Always offer God our praise.

Difficulties​

  • Chapter 7. The concept of Melchizedek, the high priest during Abraham’s time. Stated he was “without father or mother, without genealogy, without beginning of days or end of life...” v7:3. This is called a theophany (appearance of a god to a human). Not sure how to interpret this.

  • Was not an eyewitness to Jesus
  • Written before 70 AD
  • Theme: Jesus Christ is greater than any angel, priest, or Old Convenant practice

Bible Project​

https://bibleproject.com/guides/book-of-hebrews/

Background

  • The author knows the audience well; they were well-versed in the Hebrew Bible.
  • They were facing persecution and imprisonment.
  • Compares Jesus to:
    • Angels and the Torah - Jesus is superior to angels.
    • Moses and the promised land - Jesus leads His people like Moses
    • Priests and Melchizedek - Jesus is the ultimate priest and mediator
    • Sacrifices and the covenant - Jesus’s death on the cross was a permanent sacrifice.
  • Two main goals:
    • Show that Jesus is superior.
    • Encourage them to remain faithful.

1-2: Jesus Compared to Angels and the Torah

  • Jesus is God
  • Torah was delivered by Angels
  • Superior to Angels
  • Warning - pay attention to the message delivered by the Son of God

3-4: Jesus Compared to Moses and the Promised Land

  • Superior to Moses
  • Also, the leader of God’s people
  • Warning - don't rebel like the Israelites

5-7: Jesus Compared to the Priests and Melchizedek

  • Ultimate Priest
  • Morally flawless; something more was needed
  • Not from the line of Aron; Jesus from the line of Mechizedek (mel-cheese.ze.dick)
  • Warning - don’t reject Jesus; only chance

8-10: Jesus Compared to Sacrifices and the Covenant

  • His sacrifice, once for all, was vastly superior to any animal sacrifices offered in the temple
  • Warning - don’t walk away from God's gracious offer of forgiveness

11-13: An Encouragement to Stay Faithful to Jesus

  • Follow all of the great models of faith
  • Remain faithful to Jesus in spite of facing persecution

Bible Ref​

https://www.bibleref.com/hebrews

  • Author unknown. Barnabas, Silas, Appollos, etc. The most common opinion is that Paul wrote it.
  • Most likely written to Jewish Christians before 70 AD (before the destruction of the temple).
    • Likely between 64-68 during Nero's persecution of Christians in Rome.
  • There are 5 main themes:
    • The superiority of Jesus
    • The priesthood of Jesus
    • The ministry of Jesus, a priest
    • The many privileges believers have through Jesus
    • The behaviors important to living for Jesus

Melchizedek​

https://www.gotquestions.org/Melchizedek.html

pronounce as mel.KIZ.uh.dek

  • King of Salem (Jerusalem) and priest of the Most High God.
  • Gave a blessing to Abraham.
  • Abraham presented a tithe of all items he had gathered.
  • Some propose that Melchizedek was actually a pre-incarnate appearance of Jesus Christ.
  • Hebrews 7:3 says that Melchizedek was “without father or mother, without genealogy, without beginning of days or end of life, resembling the Son of God, he remains a priest forever.” The question is whether the author of Hebrews means this actually or figuratively.

  • Hebrews 4:12: "For the word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing to the division of soul and of spirit, of joints and of marrow, and discerning the thoughts and intentions of the heart."

For we do not have a high priest who is unable to sympathize with our weaknesses, but one who in every respect has been tempted as we are, yet without sin. Let us then with confidence draw near to the throne of grace, that we may receive mercy and find grace to help in time of need."
  • Hebrews 11:1: "Now faith is the assurance of things hoped for, the conviction of things not seen."
  • Hebrews 12:1–2: "Therefore, since we are surrounded by so great a cloud of witnesses, let us also lay aside every weight, and sin which clings so closely, and let us run with endurance the race that is set before us, looking to Jesus, the founder and perfecter of our faith, who for the joy that was set before him endured the cross, despising the shame, and is seated at the right hand of the throne of God."
